What it is

NowRaspberry Pi 5 on the bench — chosen for development speed, not as a product decision.
TargetRaspberry Pi Zero 2W, compact enough to hold in one hand.
DisplayWaveshare 2.7" e‑ink, 176×264, 1‑bit. Roughly 1.5 s to refresh.
Printer58 mm thermal, ESC/POS, 384 px wide, 9600 baud.
ControlsFour GPIO buttons. No touchscreen, no menus worth the name yet.
PowerInternal LiPo + charger board. Must sustain the printer's ~1.5–2 A burn peaks without sagging.

The gap that matters

The Zero 2W is roughly 5× slower per core with 512 MB of RAM against the Pi 5's multiple gigabytes. Two things in the current code have not paid for that yet:

  • The capture loop spins flat out, capturing 1640×1232 and dithering in Python, then discards almost every frame because the e‑ink cannot keep up.
  • 512 MB is not much once camera buffers and image intermediates are live at that resolution.

Neither is fixed. Both are deliberately deferred until the capture→print path is frozen — see Product direction.

Open on hardware

The serial port conflict

The printer needs a stable UART. On a Zero 2W the good one is claimed by Bluetooth, and Bluetooth and WiFi both have to stay usable — so the clean fix is off the table. The fallback UART's baud drifts with CPU frequency unless the clock is pinned, and a drifting baud rate is exactly what corrupted bitmaps into kanji the first time. To be tested on hardware, not decided on paper.
