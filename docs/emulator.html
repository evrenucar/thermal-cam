Why the panel and the paper differ

Same photo, two very different targets — and the difference is faithful to main.py, which builds them on separate paths.

prepare_for_display — scaled to fit inside 176×264 and centred on white, so a frame that isn't 2:3 gets white bars. About 46k pixels.
prepare_for_printer — scaled so the width is 384, height follows. No letterboxing, no crop. Often 4× more pixels than the panel.

So the paper shows more detail and no white bars, while the panel is a low-resolution preview of the same framing. Both are dithered with Floyd–Steinberg, but dithering ~46k pixels and ~200k pixels from the same image produces visibly different grain.
